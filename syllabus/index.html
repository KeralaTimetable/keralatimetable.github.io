Frequently Asked Questions — KTU 2024 First Year Syllabus

How do I know which KTU 2024 Group (A–D) my branch belongs to?

You don't need to memorize it—just select your Semester and Branch above and we automatically map it for you. For reference: Group A = CSE, IT, AI & DS, Cyber Security; Group B = ECE, EEE, EIE, Robotics, Biomedical; Group C = Civil, Mechanical, Chemical, Automobile, Mechatronics, Industrial, Aeronautics, Agriculture, Naval Arch, Polymer, Safety; Group D = Biotechnology & Food Technology.

Why do Physics, Chemistry, Health & Wellness, and Life Skills show in both S1 and S2?

Courses with KTU 2024 codes ending in 12x (121, 122, 127, 128) are shared across Semester 1 and Semester 2 depending on your college's first-year batch split.

Are these PDFs officially extracted from KTU 2024 Scheme books?

Yes. Every single subject PDF is cleanly split page-for-page from APJAKTU's official B.Tech 2024 First Year syllabus PDFs with cover pages and blank dividers removed.
