Frequently Asked Questions — KTU 2024 First Year Syllabus

Which KTU 2024 Scheme Group does my B.Tech branch belong to?

Group A covers CSE, IT, AI & DS, Cyber Security, and CSBS. Group B covers ECE, EEE, EIE, Robotics, and Biomedical. Group C covers Civil, Mechanical, Chemical, Automobile, Mechatronics, Industrial, Aeronautics, Agriculture, Naval Architecture, Polymer, and Safety. Group D covers Biotechnology, Biochemical Engineering, and Food Technology.

Why do some subjects show both S1 and S2 badges?

Courses with KTU 2024 codes ending in 12x (Physics 121, Chemistry 122, Health & Wellness 127, and Life Skills 128) are shared across Semester 1 and Semester 2 depending on your college's batch split.

What is the CIE and ESE mark split in KTU 2024 Scheme?

Most theory and theory+lab courses carry 40 CIE + 60 ESE marks (2 hrs 30 min exam), while Entrepreneurship & IPR (UCEST206) has 60 CIE + 40 ESE, labs carry 50 CIE + 50 ESE (internal), and activity courses (UCHUT128, UCHWT127/UCPWT127) are 100% continuous internal evaluation.
