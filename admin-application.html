Community Admin Application

Help us protect and guide the Kerala Timetable student community. Keep our spaces spam-free, highly useful, and focused on student success.

The Core Principle

"Admin is a responsibility, not a status."

Use the position to help students, not to gain personal advantage.

Official Admin Rules

  • 1.
    Student First & Neutrality Always prioritize the benefit and safety of students. Communicate respectfully and avoid unnecessary arguments.
  • 2.
    No Abuse of Power Privileges must not be used for personal benefit, promotions, referral links, spam, harassment, or targeting members.
  • 3.
    Protect Member Privacy & Data Never share members' phone numbers, messages, screenshots, or personal information outside the community. No scraping or data selling.
  • 4.
    Responsible Moderation & Teamwork Scams, spam, and suspicious DMs should be handled swiftly. Discuss major bans or announcements with the existing admin team first.
  • 5.
    Keep Internal Matters Internal Admin discussions and moderation decisions should not be unnecessarily shared outside the admin team.
