Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 4, 2026

At Kerala Timetable (accessible from https://keralatimetable.in), the privacy and safety of our student visitors and advertising partners is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected, recorded, and utilized by Kerala Timetable and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect and Third-Party Services

While we do not require students to create user accounts to browse our academic platform, we utilize reliable third-party infrastructure and collect specific information only when you voluntarily interact with our features:

  • Academic Downloads, Question Papers & Cloud Storage: Our study materials, notes, syllabi, and Previous Year Questions (PYQs) are securely hosted via Google Drive and our official cloud APIs. When you view or download a resource, you may interact with Google's infrastructure, which is governed by Google’s standard privacy terms. When students voluntarily submit question papers through our upload portal, no personal identity is publicly attached to the document.
  • Sponsored Advertisements & Razorpay Payment Processing: When brands, educators, or college event organizers book an advertising slot via our Advertise With Us page, we collect campaign details including your Company/Event Name, Advertisement Category, Preferred Schedule, and WhatsApp Number. All payments are processed securely through Razorpay (supporting UPI, Cards, and NetBanking). Kerala Timetable never stores your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank credentials on our servers. Campaign booking details are used strictly to verify your payment and coordinate your ad placement via WhatsApp within 12 hours.
  • Campus Ambassador Program: When you apply for our Campus Ambassador Program, we collect personal information including your Name, WhatsApp Number, and College Name. This data is used strictly for program coordination, internal verification, and outreach, and will never be shared or sold.
  • Vortex eFootball Tournament: When you register for the Vortex eFootball Tournament hosted by Kerala Timetable, we collect necessary participant data such as gaming IDs, contact information, and payment reference numbers. This data is used strictly for tournament bracketing, verification, and prize distribution. We guarantee that this collected data is never shared, sold, or distributed to any third party under any circumstances.
  • Polls & Trend Radar: We use Google Firebase to handle and store response data from our live interactive student polls and exam review trackers. This community feedback data is collected anonymously and is never tied back to an individual user identity.
  • Browser Push Notifications: We offer opt-in push notifications via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to send instant university updates, status history alerts, and timetable changes. Firebase manages anonymous notification tokens to deliver these alerts. You can opt out or disable notifications at any time directly through your web browser's settings.
  • Log Files: Like standard websites, Kerala Timetable utilizes automated log files. The information inside includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These are strictly used for server performance metrics and security, and are not linked to personally identifiable information.

2. WhatsApp Community Privacy & Guidelines

Kerala Timetable operates an official WhatsApp community serving 6,000+ active KTU students to provide instant university announcements. To maintain a focused and secure environment, the following rules apply:

  • 🔴 Important Privacy Notice: Taking phone numbers from our WhatsApp groups to direct message (DM) members without their explicit consent is strictly prohibited. This is a secure, monitored educational community, and anyone found bothering or harassing others will be instantly and permanently removed.
  • Geographic Restrictions: To ensure community security and relevance, phone numbers registered outside of India (e.g., country codes such as +1) are not permitted. Accounts utilizing international virtual numbers will not be accepted into the community and are subject to immediate removal.
  • Strictly Academic Discussions: Group discussions must remain exclusively focused on APJAKTU-related academics, exams, and university matters. General members are strictly prohibited from posting off-topic content, spam, or unauthorized advertisements.
  • Approved Partner Promotions: While unauthorized member advertisements are prohibited, community administrators may share verified, paid student opportunities booked through our official advertising portal. Kerala Timetable acts solely as a distributor for these opportunities (see Section 8 for details).
  • Reporting Unwanted Contact: If you receive any unsolicited DMs from a group member, please report the number directly to the group administrators. We will take immediate action and keep your identity completely confidential.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Kerala Timetable uses standard browser cookies and `localStorage` to optimize user experience. These store visitor preferences (such as dark/light theme settings, saved syllabus filters, and dismissed notification banners). This data remains on your device and allows us to optimize page load speeds and customize our front-end interface.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google may act as a third-party advertising vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve advertisements to our site visitors based upon their visits to keralatimetable.in and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons within their respective advertisements and links that may appear on Kerala Timetable, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note: Kerala Timetable has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

6. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Kerala Timetable's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers, payment gateways (such as Razorpay), cloud providers (such as Google Drive), or external university portals. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party services for more detailed information on their practices.

7. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. Kerala Timetable is designed for university engineering students and does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.
