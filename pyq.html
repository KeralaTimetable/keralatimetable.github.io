Frequently Asked Questions — KTU PYQ Finder

How do I find and download KTU B.Tech previous year question papers PDF?

Enter your KTU subject code (such as GAMAT101, UCEST105, MAT101, CST201, or MET204) or subject name into the KTU PYQ Finder search box, or switch to the Browse Semester tab to filter by Scheme (2024, 2019, 2015), Department/Branch (CSE, ECE, ME, CE, EEE), and Semester (S1 to S8). You can preview any paper online or download the PDF directly.

Does KTU PYQ Finder include 2024 Scheme, 2019 Scheme, and 2015 Scheme question papers?

Yes. Kerala Timetable's KTU PYQ Finder indexes APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (APJAKTU) B.Tech regular and supplementary exam question papers across the 2024 Scheme (including S1 and S2 courses like GAMAT101, GAMAT201, GAPHT121, GXCYT122, GXEST203, GXEST204, UCEST105, and UCEST206), the 2019 Scheme (S1 to S8), and the 2015 Scheme.

Can I combine multiple KTU previous year question papers into one PDF?

Yes. Tick the checkbox on any number of regular or supplementary question papers in your search results, or click Select All, then press Merge & Download in the bottom dock to combine all selected exam years into a single organized PDF file.
