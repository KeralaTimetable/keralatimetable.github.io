When do the B.Tech S7 (2019 Scheme) exams start and end?
The B.Tech S7 (Regular & Supplementary) November 2026 examinations begin on Monday, November 23, 2026 (Slot A, 9:30 AM – 12:30 PM) and conclude on Wednesday, December 2, 2026 (Slot D, MCN401 Industrial Safety Engineering). Does this timetable include Working Professionals (WP)?
Yes, per official Notification No. KTU/SOEX-IV/2122/2024 dated 07.11.2026, this schedule covers B.Tech S7 (R, S) students including Working Professionals (R, S) under the 2019 Scheme.
