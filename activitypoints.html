Official KTU Handbook Guidelines (Section 2)

1. Participation vs. Winning (Rule i)

In the 2024 Scheme, participation and winning points cannot be combined for the same event—only the highest level of achievement attained is counted. (In the 2019 Scheme, Sports/Cultural winners receive additional bonus points up to 60 pts, or 80 pts for Level IV/V).

2. Mandatory for Degree Award (Rules iv & v)

Earning the required Activity Points (3 Credits in 2024 Scheme / 2 Credits in 2019 Scheme) is compulsory. Final semester results may be withheld until minimum points are achieved. Activity Credits are not included in SGPA/CGPA.

3. Approved Skilling Courses (Code 3.17)

Points are awarded at 1 point per hour for courses notified by KTU (in association with K-DISC, SWAYAM/NPTEL, and IIT Bombay Spoken Tutorial). No points are awarded for unapproved courses.

4. Personal Activity File & SFA Verification (Rules vi–viii)

Only activities completed during the B.Tech study period are eligible. Every student must maintain a Personal Activity Points File and submit documentary proofs to the Senior Faculty Advisor (SFA) at the end of every semester.
