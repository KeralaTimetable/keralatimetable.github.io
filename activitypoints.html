Important Rules to Remember

1. Equal Points in All 3 Groups (2024 Scheme)

Regular students need 40 points in Group I, 40 points in Group II, and 40 points in Group III (Total 120 points = 3 credits). Extra points in one group won't carry over to another group.

2. Participation vs. Winner Points

If you win a prize in an event, you get the Winner points (higher value). You cannot add both Participation and Winner points for the same competition.

3. Online Skilling Courses (Code 3.17)

KTU-approved skilling courses give 1 Activity Point per hour of course duration (up to 40 points in Group III).

4. How to Submit Certificates

Keep all original certificates in your Personal Activity File and submit them to your Senior Faculty Advisor (SFA) at the end of each semester.
