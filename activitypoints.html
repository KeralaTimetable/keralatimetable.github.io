Official KTU Handbook Rules & Verification

Balanced 3-Group Requirement (2024 Scheme)

Regular students must earn 120 points total with at least 40 points in each of Group I, Group II, and Group III (1 credit per group = 3 credits). Excess points in one group cannot compensate for a shortfall in another group.

Participation + Winner Rule

If you win a prize in an event, only the higher achievement (Winner points) is counted—participation and winning points for the same event cannot be combined. Only the highest level achieved is awarded.

Approved Skilling Courses (Code 3.17)

University-approved skilling courses carry 1 Activity Point per hour of course duration (up to 40 points max). Keep your completion certificate with verifiable QR/URL ready for your Senior Faculty Advisor (SFA).

Personal Activity File & SFA Signature

Only activities completed during your B.Tech admission period are valid (e.g., Four-Wheeler Driving License must be issued after joining). Submit original certificates to your SFA at the end of every semester.
