Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the Student Portal not opening?

During peak announcement windows (such as B.Tech semester results or revaluation schedules), APJAKTU servers face severe traffic spikes, leading to 504 Gateway Timeouts or connection resets.

How does this live ping tool work?

Our cloud monitor dispatches active HTTP/HTTPS HEAD requests directly against official university gateways to measure true response latency and detect server-side outages in real time.
